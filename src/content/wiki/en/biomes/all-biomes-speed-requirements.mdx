## How many biomes does Steal an Egg have?

Twelve: Forest, Lake, Desert, Jungle, Snow, Volcano, Abyss Ocean, Prehistoric, Cosmic, Cherry Blossom, Titan Temple, and Angels & Demons. Each one past Forest is gated by a minimum Speed threshold, and each has its own Guardian(s) defending its egg nests — generally, higher biomes hold higher-value eggs but respond faster to a failed steal.

## Why is the jump into Cosmic so much bigger than the ones before it?

Going from Jungle (40,000 Speed) to Cosmic (roughly 700 million) is a jump of nearly **17,500x** — nothing else in the progression comes close. Practically, this means the mid-game biomes (Snow through Prehistoric) function as your main earning-and-training grounds for a long stretch before Cosmic becomes realistic; don't expect to walk into Cosmic shortly after clearing Jungle.

## Does a higher biome always mean a better egg?

Generally yes, but not automatically worth the risk. A biome's Guardians scale with its value, so arriving with only the bare minimum Speed threshold — rather than a comfortable margin above it — usually means getting caught often enough that the biome isn't actually profitable yet. See the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) for the mistake of pushing too early.

## Where should I actually be earning while I train toward the next biome?

Whichever biome you can currently clear comfortably, not the one you're saving up to reach. See the [pet tier list](/tier-list/pet-tier-list/) for which pets are worth prioritizing once a new biome opens up, since not every egg in a newly unlocked biome is actually worth farming over your current spot. For the training itself, see the [Speed and Treadmill guide](/guides/speed-and-treadmill-guide/).
