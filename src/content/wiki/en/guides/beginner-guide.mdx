## What's the core loop in Steal an Egg?

Every session boils down to four steps: **sneak into a nest and steal an egg → race it back to your base while avoiding the Guardian and rival players who can rob you mid-carry → hatch the egg into a pet that earns cash automatically → spend that cash on Treadmill Speed** to move faster and unlock access to tougher biomes with better eggs. Everything else in the game is a variation on this loop.

## How do I get started as a brand-new player?

Before your first run, like and favorite the game and join its official Roblox group — this grants a **one-time 10,000 Speed bonus** that most guides forget to mention, and it meaningfully shortens the early grind. After that, start in the **Forest** biome: it has no Speed requirement, its Guardians are forgiving for a fresh account, and its eggs still pay well enough to fund your first upgrades.

## How should I balance stealing versus training?

A commonly cited ratio is **three egg-farming runs for every one Treadmill session** — enough to keep cash flowing in while still steadily raising your Speed. You also can't steal eggs at night in this game, so treat that downtime window as your designated Treadmill session instead of idling.

## Do cosmetic Trails actually matter?

Yes — Trails aren't purely cosmetic here. Only your **currently equipped** Trail contributes to your Speed multiplier, so there's no benefit to owning several; save toward the best one you can afford rather than buying every incremental tier. The strongest currently available is the **Divine Trail at a 14x multiplier**.

## What mistakes slow new players down the most?

- **Chasing rarity before you're ready.** An egg from a harder biome is a bad target if its Guardian catches you almost every run — that's lost time, not progress.
- **Pushing to the bare minimum Speed threshold.** Build a safety margin above a biome's requirement before you attempt it regularly; running at the exact minimum means one bad server tick costs you the egg.
- **Spending every coin instantly.** Keep a small cash reserve so an upgrade you want isn't stalled the moment it becomes available.
- **Prioritizing the flashiest egg over a clean delivery.** A safely delivered common egg beats a rare one you lose to a rival halfway home.
