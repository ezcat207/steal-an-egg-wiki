## What does Speed actually control?

Speed is the stat that gates which biomes you can access — each biome past the Forest starter zone has a minimum Speed threshold, and you can't enter until you clear it. It also affects how fast you move while carrying a stolen egg, which matters directly for outrunning Guardians and rival players.

## How do I raise my Speed?

Mainly through the **Treadmill**, funded by cash your hatched pets earn passively. A commonly used pacing rule is **three egg-farming runs for every one Treadmill session** — enough to keep your income growing while your Speed climbs steadily in parallel, rather than stalling one to max the other.

## Is there a way to train at "off" hours?

Yes — you **cannot steal eggs at night** in Steal an Egg, so that nightly downtime is the natural window to put into Treadmill training instead of waiting it out idle.

## Do Trails actually affect Speed, or are they cosmetic?

They affect Speed. Trails are visually cosmetic, but whichever one you have **equipped** also applies a Speed multiplier — importantly, only the equipped Trail counts, not every Trail you've unlocked. That means there's no reason to buy every incremental tier; it's more efficient to save toward the strongest one you can afford. The best currently available is the **Divine Trail, at a 14x multiplier**.

## What's a realistic new-player Speed head start?

New accounts can claim a **one-time 10,000 Speed bonus** just for liking and favoriting the game and joining its official Roblox group — see the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) for the full onboarding steps. It's a meaningful shortcut through the slowest early grinding and costs nothing.
