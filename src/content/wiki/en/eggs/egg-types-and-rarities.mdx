## How many rarity tiers are there?

Ten, from **Common** up through **Divine** at the top. Across Steal an Egg's 12 standard biomes there are roughly **104 biome-hatchable pets** distributed through that ladder, and on top of those, **53 additional event-exclusive pets** come from limited-time egg types: Brainrot, Monster, Parasite, and Rift eggs — none of which count toward the standard rarity tiers.

## What are the top-end Divine pets, and where do they come from?

Divine-tier pets are generally tied to a specific biome's own Divine Egg:

- **Unicorn** — Cosmic biome
- **Kitsune** — Cherry Blossom biome
- **Nightflame** — Titan Temple biome
- **Dreadscale** — from Monster Chests (event)
- **Shattered Colossus** — from the Rift event (requires sacrificing three other pets)

See the [pet tier list](/tier-list/pet-tier-list/) for how these actually rank by earnings once hatched.

## What affects how long an egg takes to hatch?

Rarity is the biggest factor, but it's not the only one — an egg's **size, weight, and any active mutation or hatching boost** also shift the timer. In practice, hatch times range from a few seconds for common eggs to **24+ hours or more** for the rarest ones, so timing when you steal a top-tier egg matters if you want to be online when it finishes.

## Do rare eggs spawn on a fixed schedule?

Not predictably from a player's perspective. Secret, Eternal, and Divine-tier eggs can appear following a **server reset**, and because they're valuable, their appearance is typically flagged server-wide — which also means every other player on the server knows exactly where to go steal it from you.

## Where can I check the exact drop table for a specific biome?

Per-egg drop tables (which biome drops which specific eggs) are tracked by community wikis and change as the game updates — treat any specific per-egg percentage you see elsewhere as unofficial and subject to change.
