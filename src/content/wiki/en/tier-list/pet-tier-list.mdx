## What determines a pet's ranking in this tier list?

Money earned per second, since that's the resource that funds every other upgrade in the game (Treadmill Speed, Security Defense Wall). This list uses community-tracked figures compiled by third-party trackers rather than official developer numbers — Roblox simulators like this one patch balance often, so treat rankings as directional and re-verify before a big commitment.

## SS Tier — the ceiling, with real costs attached

- **Shattered Colossus** — roughly **$3.5B/second**, the highest tracked rate in the game. Locked to the Rift event and obtained by sacrificing three other pets at the Riftborn Banner — a genuinely expensive pick, not a casual goal.
- **Kitsune** — roughly **$3.2B/second**, from the Cherry Blossom biome's Divine Egg.
- **Nightflame** — roughly **$3B/second**, from the Titan Temple biome's Divine Egg.

## S Tier — the realistic endgame target

- **Unicorn** — roughly **$1B/second** (Cosmic biome).
- **Eternal Lunar Dragon** — roughly **$250M/second**.
- **Mosasaurus** — roughly **$180M/second**.
- **El Maja** — roughly **$130M/second**.

Every pet in this tier clears at least **$30M/second**, which is where most players should actually be aiming — the SS tier above it comes with either event-locking or steep sacrifice costs.

## Which top-tier pets are worth prioritizing long-term?

**Kitsune and Unicorn** stand out as the most reliable picks specifically because both drop from their own standing Divine Eggs rather than a time-limited event — meaning they stay obtainable and don't risk becoming permanently unavailable once an event ends, unlike Shattered Colossus.
